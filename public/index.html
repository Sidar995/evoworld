How this world works

Herbivores eat plants and flee sensed carnivores. Hungry carnivores hunt herbivores. Rainfall changes plant growth: Dry 2, Normal 8, Wet 16 patches per second.

Speed and sensing are inherited with fixed small mutations. Wider sensing and faster movement cost energy. Carnivores pay 1.25× maintenance and receive 80% of captured prey energy.

Reproduction needs energy 90, age 10 seconds, and a 10-second cooldown. A parent gives 35 energy and pays 5 more. Maximum age is 180 seconds. Each parent has at most six offspring: an artificial model restriction.

Generations overlap. Category extinction is valid. Runs stop at 30 simulated minutes or all-animal extinction; reaching 300 animals pauses the world.

Keyboard inspection: focus the world, press Enter to select the first living animal, use arrow keys to cycle by ID, and Escape to clear.
